📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Stella Maris College and Officers IAS Academy have jointly launched a two-year civil services coaching programme to prepare aspirants for UPSC examinations.

The programme offers comprehensive coaching in General Studies (GS) and Civil Services Aptitude Test (CSAT), combining two offline sessions and an online session every week. It aims to strengthen conceptual understanding, analytical abilities, aptitude skills, and current affairs awareness among aspirants, a release stated.

The programme provides aspirants a structured pathway to prepare for the exams while keeping up their academic or professional commitments, according to Sr. Stella Mary, Principal, Stella Maris College.

Classes will be held between 5 p.m. and 7.30 p.m. and will commence from September 24. For registration and more details contact 9633448902/9363730226.
