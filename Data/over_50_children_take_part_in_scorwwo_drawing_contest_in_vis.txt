📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

The South Coast Railway Women’s Welfare Organisation (SCoRWWO) held an “On-the-Spot Drawing and Painting Competition” in Visakhapatnam on Sunday, (September 21, 2026) SCoRWWO president Charu Mathur led the event.

More than 50 children participated. The competition was meant to give them a platform for creative expression, the organisers said.

The dignitaries praised the participants for their artwork and original thinking. Gifts and health kits were distributed to all the children.
