📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Bheemunipatnam MLA Ganta Srinivasa Rao and Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC) Commissioner Ketan Garg on Monday inspected the arrangements at the V-Convention Centre in Madhurawada ahead of Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu’s virtual launch of key development projects.

The Chief Minister will virtually inaugurate 16 infrastructure projects—13 under the GVMC and 3 under the Visakhapatnam Metropolitan Region Development Authority (VMRDA)—from Kuppam on Tuesday as part of the Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) programme.

The two-hour event, scheduled between 10:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m, will be accessible to general public through digital screens and high-definition LED displays across the venue.
