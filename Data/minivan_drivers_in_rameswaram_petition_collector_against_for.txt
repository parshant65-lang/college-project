📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Rameswaram Minivan Drivers’ Association submitted a petition to Ramanathapuram District Collector M. Sivaguru Prabhakaran on Monday, opposing a Forest Department order restricting their entry into key tourist destinations.

R. Chandrasekaran, a petitioner and local driver, said the recent directive barred petrol- and diesel-powered vans and cars from carrying passengers from Rameswaram railway station to Dhanushkodi and Arichalmunai. He said the government planned to introduce CNG-powered buses on the routes to curb pollution.

Questioning the manner in which the order was issued, Mr. Chandrasekaran said, “When over 200 families depend directly on this livelihood, how can such an order be issued overnight without any prior notice?” He said more than 200 auto-rickshaws also operated along the route.

He said drivers had been navigating the challenging sand and seawater terrain in the region since the early 2000s and that archival footage of their operations was available online. He said the drivers, who had depended on the trade for years, were concerned about losing their livelihood following the order.

Questioning the environmental rationale for the restriction, Mr. Chandrasekaran said, “If pollution is the real concern, what about the diesel-operated boats and trawlers? Would they not harm the sea and natural resources?”

The association urged the District Collector to intervene, withdraw the restrictive order and protect the livelihood of local drivers.
