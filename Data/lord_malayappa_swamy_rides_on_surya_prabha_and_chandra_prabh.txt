📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

The seventh day of the annual Brahmotsavams of Lord Venkateswara unfolded in a vibrant blend of devotion and tradition on Monday, with Lord Malayappa Swamy taken out in procession on the majestic Surya Prabha Vahanam along the four Mada streets of Tirumala.

Devotees raised devotional chants as the processional deity passed through the streets. Traditional artistes accompanying the procession performed chekka bhajans and kolatam, while the strains of mangala vadyams added to the festive atmosphere.

The Surya Prabha Vahanam, symbolising the Sun, is traditionally associated with health, vitality and energy. Devotees believe that a darshan of Lord Malayappa Swamy on the vahanam brings health, prosperity, happiness and well-being.

TTD Executive Officer Muddada Ravichandra, Additional Executive Officer Ch. Venkaiah Chowdary and Chief Vigilance and Security Officer K.V. Muralikrishna, besides senior pontiffs, board members and other officials, participated in the vahana seva.

After noon, priests performed Snapana Thirumanjanam at the Ranganayakula Mandapam inside the temple, giving Lord Malayappa Swamy and his two consorts a ceremonial bath.

In the evening, Tirumala witnessed the traditional Chatra Sthapana, or Golden Umbrella Ceremony, held on the eve of the Rathotsavam scheduled for Tuesday.

The centuries-old ceremony is conducted under the supervision of Kalyana Katta staff. Historical records trace its continuation to the descendants of Pantulugari, in accordance with a Dharmashasanam issued by Chandragiri ruler Srinivasa Mahadeva Rayalu during the reign of Sri Krishnadevaraya. The offering of the golden umbrella has continued since 1952.

The ceremony was led by Ramanathan, a descendant of Pantulugari. TTD officials and Kalyana Katta staff participated in the ritual.

The day concluded with the Chandraprabha Vahana procession at night, with thousands of devotees turning up despite intermittent showers.
