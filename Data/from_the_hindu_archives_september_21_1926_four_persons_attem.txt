📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Rugby, September 20: No less than 4 persons are or have been trying to swim the Channel to-day. They are all British. Miss Mona MacLellan and Mister Horace Carey entered the water at Cape Grisnez at 5:30 yesterday evening.

Colonel Freyberg who won the Victoria Cross made his third effort leaving Cape Grisnez at 5:30 this morning. Miss Marriott is starting from Cape Grisnez this evening.

Colonel Freyberg gave up his attempt when about seven miles from Dover owing to his position being unfavourable. He had been in water ten hours. This evening there was some excitement in Dover when a motor-boat accompanying Miss MacLellan and Horace Carey was sighted about a mile off the Dover Pier proceeding in a westerly direction. Apparently there was only one person in water but it was impossible to ascertain whether the swimmer was Miss MacLellan or Carey.
