📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

New Delhi, September 20: Leading manufacturers of toothpastes and other toilet goods agreed to-day to apply for certification by the Indian Standards Institution to ensure the quality of their products.

They also assured the Union Minister of State for Civil Supplies and Co-operation, Mr. A. C. George, that they would within a short time make their suggestions about a machinery which could deal with the pricing of these products.

Mr. George had a meeting here with senior functionaries of the All-India Soap and Toiletries Makers’ Association, which represents manufacturers of toothpastes, soaps, cosmetics, perfumes, etc. in the organised sector.

Later, speaking to newsmen, Mr. George said there was a crisis of confidence among consumers over the claims made by manufacturers of toothpastes, etc. about the quality of their products.
