📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

The water level in Papanasam Dam on Monday stood at 49.50 feet (maximum level 143 feet) with an inflow of 279.24 cusecs and a discharge of 554.75 cusecs. The level in Manimuthar Dam stood at 59.72 feet (118 feet) with an inflow of 92 cusecs and a dicharge of 50 cusecs.
