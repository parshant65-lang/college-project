📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

A micro job fair organised by the District Employment and Career Guidance Centre, Coimbatore, will be held at the Thavathiru Santhalinga Adigalar Tamil College campus in Perur on September 25.

The fair will be held from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Candidates who have completed Class 10, Class 12, ITI, diploma, undergraduate or engineering courses can participate with their resumes and copies of educational certificates.

There is no age limit and no registration fee. Several private companies are expected to participate and recruit candidates for various vacancies. Selected candidates will be issued appointment orders on the spot.

Candidates seeking to participate can register through the Tamil Nadu private jobs portal and the National Career Service portal. For details, contact 0422-2642388.

Collector Pavankumar G. Giriyappanavar appealed to jobseekers to participate in the fair and make use of the opportunity.
