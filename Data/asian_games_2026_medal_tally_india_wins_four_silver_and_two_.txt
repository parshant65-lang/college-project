📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Asian Games 2026 in Japan’s Aichi-Nagoya saw India win four silver medals and two bronze at the international sporting event.

A silver was bagged by the Women’s 10 M Air Rifle team comprising Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Maskar, and Vidarsa Vinod won a silver.

Elavenil Valarivan also won another in Women’s 10 M Air Rifle Individual.

Rudrankksh Patil, Himanshu Dhillon, and Parth Mane won a silver in men’s 10 M Air Rifle team.

Himanshu Dhillon also won a silver in Men’s 10 M Air Rifle Individual.

The two bronze medals were won by Rudranksh Patil for Men’s 10 M Air Rifle Individual, Suchika Tariyal in Women’s Traditional MMA 60 kg.

India is also in the women’s cricket final event with either a gold or silver medal assured.

A contingent of 501 Indian athletes, comprising 267 men and 234 women, is competing across 36 disciplines at the 2026 Asian Games. They aim to build on a historic performance at the Hangzhou Games, where India finished fourth behind China, Japan and South Korea with an impressive haul of 106 medals.
