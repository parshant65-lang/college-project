📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Gold cards and tickets for the Dasara finale events and drone shows went on sale on Monday and can be purchased through the official Mysuru Dasara website.

As announced by the district administration, gold cards and tickets for the Jamboo Savari, Torchlight Parade and drone shows were made available from 11.30 a.m. on Monday through the official website.

According to the office of the Deputy Commissioner and Dasara Special Officer, the prices of the gold cards and tickets have been retained at last year’s rates and have not been revised. Entry will be permitted to one person per gold card or ticket. The gold card is priced at ₹6,500, while tickets for the Jamboo Savari and Torchlight Parade cost ₹3,500 and ₹1,500 per person, respectively. The tickets to the drone show have been priced at ₹1,000 per person.

The gold card provides entry to the Jamboo Savari, Torchlight Parade and one drone show, besides permitting access to the Mysuru Zoo, Mysuru Palace and Chamundi Hill.
