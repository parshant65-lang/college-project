📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Minister of Industries and Commerce T.G. Bharath on Monday (September 21, 2026) laid the foundation stone for renovation works to be undertaken at a cost of ₹14 lakh at the Mahammadiya Waqf Complex in Kurnool. The funds will be used for necessary repairs and renovations at the complex.

Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Bharath said Mahammadiya Waqf Complex had been neglected between 2019 and 2024. He said a committee was constituted for maintaining the complex after he assumed office and this created an opportunity to focus on its development.

He said the committee members are working collectively to develop the complex and emphasised that working as a team allows for the effective implementation of development initiatives. Mr. Bharath stated that in addition to the current renovation works, efforts would be made to identify and complete other development projects in a phased manner in the future.

The committee members expressed their gratitude to Mr. Bharath for his special interest in the development of the complex and his support for the renovation works.
