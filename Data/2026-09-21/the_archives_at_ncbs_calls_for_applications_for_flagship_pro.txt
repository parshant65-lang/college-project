📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

The Archives at NCBS invites applications for the second cohort of the Program for the Archiving of Science and Technology (PAST), a 12-month flagship programme that supports digital preservation and enables digital access to at-risk archival material.

As part of PAST, which focuses on under-represented and marginalised histories in science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and medicine (STEM), participants will have access to year-long training, funding support, expert input and guidelines, skill development exercises, and more.

The appllication deadline for PAST is 23 October. To know more, log into https://archives.ncbs.res.in/past27
