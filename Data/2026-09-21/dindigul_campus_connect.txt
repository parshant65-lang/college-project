📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

Kalasalingam University, Krishnankoil, celebrated its 102nd Founder’s Day.

Pro-chancellor S. Arivazhagi Sridaran presided. Research incentives of ₹33.5 lakh were distributed to 149 faculty members, while 65 received Research Competency Awards for funded projects, patents and consultancy. Teaching Excellence Awards were presented to 40 faculty members.

Academic awards were also given to the top three rank-holders among 350 students. The Annual Research Report 2025 was released on the occasion.

G.T.N. Arts College organises a one-day seminar on ‘Climate Change Mitigation’. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

Departments of Social Work and BBA, along with the Environment Club of G.T.N. Arts College, organised a one-day seminar on ‘Climate Change Mitigation’. Nearly 100 students and faculty members participated.

Rev. Fr. Mathew SDB, Director and Principal, Don Bosco Media Academy, Tiruchi, spoke on the causes and impacts of climate change. Principal P. Ravichandran urged students to adopt sustainable lifestyles and contribute to environmental conservation.

Kamaraj College of Engineering and Technology organises a Student Induction Programme. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

Kamaraj College of Engineering and Technology organised the Student Induction Programme (SIP 2026).

Principal Senthil, president Muralidharan and secretary Maheshkumar distributed prizes. Laya Sri, a student of P.S. Chidambara Nadar School and a national-level speech competition winner, was the special guest.

V.V.Vanniaperumal College for Women organises competitions for school students on Bharathiyar’s works. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

PG Department of Tamil, V.V. Vanniaperumal College for Women, organised competitions for school students on Bharathiyar’s works. About 107 students from 15 schools participated.

N. Arulmozhi, Head, Department of Tamil, Ayya Nadar Janaki Ammal College, Sivakasi, delivered a special lecture on ‘Nation and Affection in Bharathi’s Writings’. Secretary S.P.G.C. Srimurugan and principal S. Cinthana distributed prizes.

Standard Fireworks Rajaratnam College, organises a guest lecture on ‘Ozone Depletion and Climate Change’. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

Department of Geography, Standard Fireworks Rajaratnam College, organised a guest lecture on ‘Ozone Depletion and Climate Change’. K. Kannadasan, Guest Lecturer, Department of Geography, Madurai Kamaraj University, spoke on the formation of the ozone layer.

About 93 students from the Geography and History departments participated.

PSR Engineering College organises a national-level inter-collegiate technical symposium. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

PSR Engineering College, Sivakasi, organised RYVONEXA’26, a national-level inter-collegiate technical symposium.

Principal J.S. SenthilKumaar inaugurated. Rajachockalingam, Delivery Manager, Tata Consultancy Services, Bengaluru, addressed the participants on developing skills for employment and career growth. More than 150 students from various engineering colleges participated.

Sethu Bhaskara Agricultural College and Research Institute, Karaikudi, planted 16,000 saplings. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

Sethu Bhaskara Agricultural College and Research Institute, Karaikudi, planted 16,000 saplings with the participation of more than 3,000 students and volunteers.

The drive, led by chairman and correspondent Sethu Kumanan and principal W. Baby Rani, involved participants from educational institutions, government agencies, security forces and voluntary organisations.

Kundrakudi Ponnambala Adigalar, retired Madras High Court judge S. Rajeswaran, Sivaganga Collector P. Aakash and Superintendent of Police P. Abhishek Gupta participated.

Theni Nadar Saraswathi College of Engineering and Technology organises an intra-collegiate cultural festival. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

Theni Nadar Saraswathi College of Engineering and Technology inaugurated ‘Waves’26’, an intra-collegiate cultural festival. Principal C. Mathalai Sundaram welcomed.

Students were divided into 11 teams and participated in various competitions.

Actors Bala and Vinoth, Super Singer fame Dhanya Shree, singer Rajil Raj and other artistes were the special guests.
