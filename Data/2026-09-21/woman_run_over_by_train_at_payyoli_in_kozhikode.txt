📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

A 45-year-old woman was run over by a train at Payyoli in Kozhikode district on Monday (September 21, 2026). The victim was identified as N. Suja, a resident of Thikkodi. The incident took place around 11 a.m. The body was shifted to the Kozhikode District Hospital for post-mortem. Police sources said it was the locopilot who exchanged details of the incident with the police for immediate follow-up action.
