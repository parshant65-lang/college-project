📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

The Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply & Sewerage Board (HMWS&SB) has issued serious warning against water wastage, after imposing penalties in five such instances on Monday. Second time offenders will attract disconnection of water supply, it warned.

All the five penalised were residents of Jubilee Hills, and found to have been wasting water, during inspections conducted by the Board officials.

The inspections will be intensified in the coming days, and second time offenders will be penalised through disconnection of water supply, the note warned.

Using drinking water to wash floor and vehicles, leaving the tap open, and not shutting down the pump set even after overhead tank overflows are some of the instances of water wastage, as shared by the Board.

Despite awareness campaigns and warnings, the users are still found resorting to the same practices, calling for penalties. The penalties which had started with just ₹1000 per instance, have over the time risen to ₹5,000, then ₹10,000 and now ₹20,000. Still the violations continue to persist. the note said.

Leakages in the water supply network too will be plugged by officials within 24 hours of identification, and in case of dereliction of duties, action will be taken against officials and staff too. The water board’s drinking water pipeline extends to 15,000 kilometres in the city, to plug leakages in which, a special action plan is being be taken up. The officials have been directed to identify locations with frequent leakages and provide permanent solutions.
