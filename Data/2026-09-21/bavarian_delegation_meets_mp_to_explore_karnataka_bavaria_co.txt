📝 CLEAN ARTICLE BODY:

A delegation from the Bavarian State Parliament, facilitated by the State of Bavaria India Office, met Mysuru-Kodagu MP Yaduveer Krishnadatta Chamaraja Wadiyar in Mysuru on Sunday to explore opportunities for cooperation between Karnataka/Mysuru and Bavaria in sustainability, water management, agriculture and heritage conservation.

The discussions focused on climate resilience, sustainable agriculture, resource efficiency, the circular economy, academic collaboration and the green economy.

The delegation and Mr. Yaduveer exchanged views on common challenges, including drought, declining precipitation, water stress, wildfires, rapid urbanisation and pressure on infrastructure. They discussed the scope for sharing knowledge, technology and best practices in water management, waste and wastewater management, renewable energy and climate adaptation.

Sustainable agriculture was another key area of discussion, with an emphasis on resource-efficient farming, technological innovation, automation and improving productivity amid changing climatic conditions. The importance of food quality and nutrition, alongside food security, was also highlighted, according to a press release.

The participants discussed strengthening recycling systems, responsible plastic waste management and developing viable markets for recycled materials. They underlined the role of public awareness and early environmental education in promoting sustainable practices.

The meeting also explored cooperation in heritage conservation amid urban growth. The possibility of exchanging expertise with Bavarian institutions, including the Bavarian Administration of State-Owned Palaces, Gardens and Lakes, was discussed to preserve historic buildings and the character of cities while enabling responsible modernisation.

Academic and research partnerships in sustainability, water management and sustainable agriculture were also discussed. The delegation invited Mr. Yaduveer to visit Bavaria to explore institutional partnerships.

The discussions also covered sustainable tourism and agricultural transformation, with emphasis on balancing economic opportunities with environmental and infrastructure considerations.
